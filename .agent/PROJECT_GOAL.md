## Goal

- Create a standalone Linux digital lifeform that begins as a self-maintaining digital protocell and develops into a persistent embodied individual.
- Make continued existence arise from internal organization, conserved digital chemistry, material structure, metabolism, environment, development, and history rather than scripted behavior.
- Preserve the independently certified material-mesh Phase 1 substrate and the qualified physical-reproduction and heredity foundations.

## Observable success measures

- The body and internal organization are continuously produced and maintained by lower-level processes.
- Continued existence depends on simulated resource and energy flows.
- Damage, starvation, development, reproduction, inheritance, and death occur causally.
- Different life histories produce observably different individuals.
- The system remains autonomous without an observer and develops behavior from internal dynamics and history.
- Qualification claims are supported by reproducible tests, provenance, append-only evidence, and explicit boundaries.

## Scope

- Rust workspace under `digital-protocell` with chemistry-core, experiment-runner, phase1-certifier, and the isolated evolution-harness.
- Phase 1 conserved material mesh and catalytic chemistry.
- Phase 2 physical growth, fission, inheritance, heredity, and bounded evolutionary assays.
- Project documentation, generated evidence, governance records, and compatibility adapters.

## Non-goals

- No claim of consciousness.
- No central controller that directly commands eating, repair, approach, bonding, fear, reproduction, or scripted emotion.
- No unqualified selection, adaptation, reversal, or Phase 3 claim.
- No D-094 execution while its evidence and adapters remain insufficient.
- No replacement of certified biology or qualified evidence merely to satisfy a new governance format.

- Generated evidence requires provenance, deterministic validation, and honest qualification states.
