# Energy and Metabolic Limits

The assay uses D-091 reserve `R`, with expenditure recorded as `R → W`.

The zero-resource control produced no active edges and zero resource expenditure. Repeated actuation from a small reserve produced a first funded tension of `0.24786121534345265`, spent `0.007764571353075626`, then reached zero reserve and zero subsequent tension.

The funding rule scales local tension proportionally when the requested work exceeds available reserve. It cannot create free actuator work.
