# Goal alignment

## End goal

Digital Cell is intended to become a persistent embodied digital lifeform arising from causal material protocell dynamics. Regulation, sensing, development, memory, identity, and any later evolution must remain distributed, local, and physically coupled. The architecture must not collapse into a central controller, an LLM, a fitness function, a target shape, or an externally granted agent.

## Current position

Phase 1 causal chemistry, material growth, and physical fission are the scientific base. D-088 growth/fission and the accepted heredity infrastructure are historical evidence, not permission to reopen D-096 or start selection. The D-096 finite-allocation reciprocal-fitness route is closed/rejected. This directive therefore selects a developmental/sensorimotor architecture before any new evolutionary substrate.

## Alignment test

The selected architecture must make future behavior a consequence of local material state, local signals, and local actuation. A regulator may coordinate neighboring chemistry, but it may not author organism existence, bypass conservation, or decide reproduction from a scalar score. Development must be an unfolding of initial material and boundary conditions; sensing must transduce existing physical quantities; effectors must call existing bounded mechanisms.
