# Transfer and locality results

The synthetic split/merge assay uses the same mapping implementation as the live assay.

- A uniform activity field remains uniform through a local split and remains bounded through a local merge.
- A seeded local pattern maps only to nearby old vertices under the local edge-scale bound.
- Repeated replay of the same frames and event sequence is byte-identical.
- No global state redistribution or target pattern is present.

These results establish continuity of the existing field only. They do not establish memory, identity, differentiation, or learning.
