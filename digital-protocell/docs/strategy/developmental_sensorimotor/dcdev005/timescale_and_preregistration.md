# Timescale Preregistration

The fixed pair was recorded in `timescale_preregistration.json` before the
first DC-DEV-005 qualification assay.

| Quantity | Frozen value | Characteristic time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Existing regulatory decay | `0.5 / time` | `2.0` |
| Adaptation load | `0.1 / time` | `10.0` |
| Adaptation recovery | `0.05 / time` | `20.0` |

The slow trace is therefore at least five times slower than the existing fast
regulatory decay. There is no parameter screening, sweep, or post-result
tuning.
