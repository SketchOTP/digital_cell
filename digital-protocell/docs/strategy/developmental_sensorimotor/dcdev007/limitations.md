# Limitations and non-claims

This result establishes only one static-obstacle contact response whose
magnitude can depend on prior local experience. It does not establish
navigation, locomotion, avoidance, pain, fear, object recognition, resource
seeking, general learning, identity, intelligence, or evolution.

The obstacle is inert and static. There is no resource ecology, target,
semantic interpretation, action selector, or fitness criterion. If the active
arm fails to reduce integrated contact burden under the fixed assay, the
failure is returned rather than repaired with a new actuator or tuned horizon.
