# Causal constraints

1. **Material authority:** organism state is the conserved mesh and its approved chemistry/transport state; controller state cannot mint material.
2. **Locality:** a regulator reads only its local patch, bounded neighbors, and signals physically available through existing interfaces.
3. **No central controller:** there is no global brain, coordinator, policy server, LLM, or organism-wide hidden state that authorizes action.
4. **No target morphology:** development is measured as observed state transition and function, not distance to a designed shape.
5. **No fitness scalar:** sensorimotor regulation cannot assign birth/death or survival from a score.
6. **Conservation:** actuation is an intent into existing bounded mechanisms; it cannot bypass energy, catalyst, transport, or fission laws.
7. **Persistent state:** future regulatory state must persist locally through steps and have explicit birth/fission provenance.
8. **Reproducibility:** fixed accepted time increments, seeded local updates, append-only events, and versioned manifests are required.
9. **Failure honesty:** unsupported sensing, actuation, heredity, or development adapters fail closed and are reported as unavailable.
10. **Scope:** no selection, adaptation, reversal, population fitness, or evolutionary closure is authorized by this package.
