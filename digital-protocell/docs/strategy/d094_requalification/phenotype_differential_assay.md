# Matched phenotype differential assay

SR-004 preregistered the required causal comparison before considering a new
population campaign:

```text
H under H ecology versus B under H ecology
B under B ecology versus H under B ecology
H under neutral versus B under neutral
```

The sealed D-094R2 runner emitted terminal phenotype frequencies, descendant
contributions, fissions, generation depth, viability and lineage links. It did
not emit matched physiological endpoints such as time to fission, completed
fissions by founder type, reserve persistence, repair recovery, or phenotype-
specific survival/reproduction under each ecology. Those values are therefore
`NOT_RECORDED`.

No new differential run was authorized or executed. The reciprocal crossover
condition is consequently **not established**. This is deliberately distinct
from claiming that the Gate 5 functional phenotype never could affect an
ecology; the existing evidence only supports the stronger statement that the
causal prerequisite for a new selection campaign was not demonstrated.
