# SR-004 final conclusion

## `SR004_D094R2_VALID_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE_RECONFIRMED`

SR-004 independently reconciled the sealed D-094R2 Gate 6 evidence and
reproduced its bounded scientific conclusion: 8 H, 8 B and 8 neutral
replicates all completed generation 8, all remained viable, H had a negative
frequency effect, B was far below the frozen `0.15` requirement, and both
descendant-effect intervals crossed zero.

The sealed D-094 Gate 4 and Gate 5 artifacts also qualify mechanism-specific
heredity and functional catalytic expression. They do not provide the matched
H-versus-B physiological endpoints needed to demonstrate a reciprocal
environment-dependent advantage. D-094R2 therefore remains valid negative
selection evidence, not an invalid or zero-generation run, and no new
population-selection campaign is justified.

This conclusion does not claim that the Gate 5 functional phenotype is
logically impossible to couple to ecology; it records that the required causal
coupling was not demonstrated by the sealed evidence. Any future test would
require a separately authorized, preregistered differential assay and, if
competition is claimed, a finite conserved resource adapter.

Scientific core: unchanged. D-094 heredity: unchanged. Mutation/adaptation,
reversal and Phase 3: not run and not authorized.
