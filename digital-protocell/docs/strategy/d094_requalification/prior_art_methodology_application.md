# Prior-art methodology applied

SR-002 findings were used as design checks rather than as a mandate to rerun:

- **Evo²Sim:** resource limitation and ecological turnover must be explicit;
  the D-094R2 common clock is not relabeled as finite competition.
- **Avida:** replicate identity, neutral controls and generation accounting
  must be explicit; the 8/8/8 seed-matched result is preserved.
- **Aevol:** lineage and descendant contribution are retained as separate
  measurements; terminal phenotype frequency alone is not treated as fitness.
- **MABE2:** world, organism and observer responsibilities remain separated;
  the D-094 observer cannot assign fitness or modify chemistry.
- **Stringmol:** hereditary state is not sufficient without function coupled to
  a measurable causal endpoint; Gate 5 functional expression is recorded, but
  the ecology differential endpoint remains missing.

These lessons support preserving the valid negative Gate 6 result and refusing
to launch a new campaign before a reciprocal causal phenotype assay exists.
