# Scientific Basis

Phase 1 implements a chemically maintained phase-separated droplet as a candidate autopoietic digital protocell.

## Foundations

- **Active droplets:** Concentrated phase with interface, exchange, fuel-driven production, waste, non-equilibrium maintenance (Cahn–Hilliard + chemical sources).
- **Chemical Organization Theory:** Organizational closure — internal processes produce components that preserve those processes.
- **No explicit membrane:** Proto-boundary via phase separation φ≈0 (medium) vs φ≈1 (structure).

## Operational definition of alive (Phase 1)

Individuality, material turnover, metabolism, organizational closure, boundary maintenance, self-repair, dependence on flux, irreversible death — all measured by `CellDetector`, never fed back into chemistry.

## References (conceptual)

Active-droplet continuum models; spatial metabolism coupled to compartment structure. This implementation synthesizes these ideas with explicit R1–R5 reaction network and intervention harness.
