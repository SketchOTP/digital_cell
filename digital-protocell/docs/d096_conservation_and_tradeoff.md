# D-096 conservation and tradeoff

Gate 1 passed: allocation sums to one without clipping or normalization;
catalyst synthesis consumes structural material and activated resource;
maintenance consumes activated resource; turnover enters waste; zero-resource
steps are rejected atomically.

Gate 2 passed five-level processing and repair assays. Expression and the
corresponding conserved flux were monotonic. Processing required local nutrient
and fuel; repair amplification required local damage.

Gate 3 passed. Processing-heavy, balanced, and repair-heavy matched organisms
showed opposing activation and structural-repair flux orderings. No tested
allocation dominated both outcomes.
