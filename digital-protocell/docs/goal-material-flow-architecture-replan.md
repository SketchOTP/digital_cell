# Goal-mode material-flow architecture replan

Status: `GOAL_AGENT_REPLAN`

This is a provisional goal-agent architecture decision. It is not an
independent Architect acceptance and must not be written as one into the
canonical project state.

## Decision

The current hard-contact and finite spatial-field material-flow compositions
are insufficient to establish resource-causal physical reproduction. The
project should replan the organism-world material interface before another
runtime execution. The next design must make environmental transfer, internal
processing, and developmental material accumulation one explicit temporal
contract.

Do not reopen CLOSURE-006 through CLOSURE-014 or search another local
active-work/contact/material-allocation formula.

## Reconstructed causal boundary

The sealed resource-to-fission audit found that the historical hard-contact
runtime fissions at step 25 in both transfer-enabled and transfer-disabled
arms, while first environmental transfer occurs later. That observation is
not resource-causal reproduction and is not counted toward the goal gate.

Route-B then supplied a finite spatial N/F field and integrated it through the
accepted runtime order:

1. finite field diffusion and edge-local exchange;
2. unchanged N/F transport ledger;
3. frozen `ReactionParams::conservative_v3` metabolism;
4. unchanged D-088 growth;
5. unchanged remesh, topology, and physical fission eligibility.

The field transferred N/F on step 1 and conserved both species exactly. The
active arm delivered `199.7860652947839` N and F, remained alive, and did not
fission. Its terminal structural mass was `5.64081802225564`, below the
unchanged `1.35 * birth_mass = 118.43990571826753` gate. The transfer-disabled
control delivered zero N/F and underwent starvation collapse without fission.

This is a valid bounded negative for Route-B, not evidence that every possible
organism-world material interface is impossible.

## Why this is an architecture boundary

The source-level growth path explains why faster or more local field delivery
is not itself a sufficient next hypothesis:

- frozen reactions process internal N/F into A/W;
- maintenance, catalyst production, membrane work, and structural work consume
  that A;
- D-088 growth uses only the remaining instantaneous local A surplus;
- the fission gate reads structural mass and physical pinch eligibility, not
  environmental transfer.

Thus Route-B proves transfer and conservation, but not a temporally persistent
resource-to-growth channel. Changing a gain, threshold, field placement,
inventory, or fission gate would be another local search and is outside the
current goal boundary.

## Existing compositions considered

### D-090 shared spatial dish

`SpatialDish::spatial_dish_step` already orders environmental supply/diffusion,
transport, reactions, growth, and fission. It is useful evidence that transfer
can precede growth, but its accepted ecology used centroid sampling and an
optional uniform supply. The D-090 ecological-timescale contract failed: the
frozen organism could not simultaneously remain viable, experience scarcity,
and apply the intended pressure before reproduction. It is therefore not a
qualified Route-A solution.

### D-091 reserve physiology

D-091 provides an existing physical A-to-R storage and reserve-funded growth
composition, and its isolated reproduction requalification passed. However,
the current M2 production runtime is explicitly `MaturationCoupledV4 / reserve
OFF`. Enabling reserve requires the stamped reserve schema, selected derived
parameters, and an explicit composition identity. That is a new M2 chemistry
composition to validate, not an already-active Route-A path that can be
silently enabled.

### Route-B finite spatial material field

Route-B is the strongest tested finite material-flow candidate so far: it uses
finite world cells, conservative diffusion, edge-local DC-DEV-008 exchange,
shared-cell allocation, and no behavioral resource input. Its exact-head result
is nevertheless negative for resource-causal fission. This closes the current
finite-field implementation family pending a materially different contract.

## Replan target

The next architecture must be specified at the material-flow boundary, not at
the motor boundary. It must answer, before code is written:

1. What finite environmental stock or boundary source owns every delivered N/F
   unit?
2. What local transport and processing compartments separate environmental
   arrival from intracellular use?
3. Which already-existing physical state carries resource material across the
   maintenance timescale without a privileged hunger/satiety flag?
4. How does the same conserved material support structural growth before the
   unchanged fission gate is evaluated?
5. What control proves that transfer occurs before, and causes, reproductive
   divergence rather than merely preserving viability?
6. How does the architecture remain local, shared, finite, rotation-equivariant,
   checkpointable, and compatible with lineage/fission accounting?

The candidate may use existing D-091 reserve only if a future bounded
composition explicitly preserves its schema, parameter provenance, material
closure, and reserve-off production boundary. It may not be introduced as an
unexamined rescue of Route-B.

## Prior-art basis

Primary nutrient-limited growth models couple extracellular diffusion and local
uptake to biomass growth rather than treating uptake as an isolated contact
event. The relevant adaptable principle is a coupled environmental transport,
cellular processing, and biomass-accumulation timescale. No biological kinetic
values are imported by this replan.

## Next-execution boundary

Before a new runtime branch, produce a source-level architecture contract and
invariant matrix for the materially different interface. The contract must
show that it is not:

- another local active-work or contact attenuation formula;
- another field-placement or inventory-size variant;
- an altered `1.35x` fission gate;
- an implicit infinite bath or unledgered source;
- a new observer/controller signal;
- a silent reserve-on production change.

If no such contract can be written while preserving the frozen chemistry,
finite conservation, and causal growth/fission semantics, stop local
material-flow implementation and report that the current organism-world
architecture is insufficient for the next goal gate.
