# D-097 timing and resource delivery

Processing catalyst and internal nutrient/fuel overlapped for `100%` of accepted
assay steps. Mean half-expression occurred at step `496`; mean 95%-of-endpoint
expression occurred at step `949`. Ordinary expression paid all synthesis,
maintenance, and turnover costs.

Internal resource exposure was positive and mean unused internal nutrient plus
fuel was `132.719627` at the endpoint. Resource delivery is therefore not the
first limitation. Continued post-pulse observation cannot repair a schema check
that disables reserve storage and reserve-funded growth.

Conclusion: no processing-expression timing mismatch and no H delivery
limitation were established.
