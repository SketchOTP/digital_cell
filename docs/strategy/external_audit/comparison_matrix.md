## Binding synthesis

No external source is a safe drop-in replacement for Digital Cell's material mesh, conserved boundary, physical growth/fission, causal heredity substrate, or certifier. The most important adaptation is the experimental harness: explicit environmental protocol, generation/parent identity, neutral controls, replicate manifests, extinction reasons, lineage export, and observer-only selection measurements. The recommended next route is a thin Digital Cell-owned harness, not an embedded external framework.
