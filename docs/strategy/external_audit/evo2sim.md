# Evo²Sim

Source: `charlesrocabert/Evo2Sim`, commit `2a5b146da280ae54c8f073a6c52f979307cfa134`, tree `421f964eaf0f0e195859412ef3c8a86d8fa7b40c`; GPL-3.0 in `LICENSE`; CMake/C++.

`Genome` implements point mutation, duplication, deletion, translocation, inversion, horizontal transfer and rearrangement. `Environment` exposes explicit variation, renewal, localization, diffusion, degradation and metabolic schemes. `Simulation::update_population` kills on death/energy/metabolite/inactivity/score/functional-genome conditions, fills gaps from local high-score neighbours, divides selected cells, updates phylogeny, and mutates parent/child. `TrophicNetwork` derives production/uptake/release relationships.

The transferable result is experimental methodology: explicit environmental regimes, time/renewal schedules, local competition, generation accounting, extinction-versus-selection separation, trophic statistics, and lineage/phylogeny export. Digital Cell has pieces of shared bath/diffusion and governed D-090–D-094 campaigns, but not a general protocol object or mature multi-generation ecology harness.

Classification: `BENCHMARK` ecology and `ADAPT` protocol methodology; `REJECT_INTEGRATION` source. GPL-3.0 and genome/cell semantics are incompatible with the material mesh. Use this source to repair controls, not to assign fitness or replace causal reproduction.
