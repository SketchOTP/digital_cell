## Parameter evidence and exact obstruction

Eight independently prepared diagnostic probes vary strain and occupancy separately. The blind estimator reads observed signed coupling force and directional rates, never `design_truth` or the supplied chemical-gradient column. It solves a full-rank two-column force design for `K,K*lambda`, then recovers theta from directional log-ratios after removing the independently specified bare chemistry. Six separately sealed probes test predictions without refitting. Nondefault synthetic recovery and rank/finite/excitation/corruption tests protect against simply returning design constants. This is numerical identifiability/recovery of the chosen model with ideal computational observations; it provides neither empirical uncertainty nor biological parameter identification.

A decisive counterexample uses states `(E,ES,ESS,Pr,S,Sm)=(.1,.05,.05,.01,1,.1)` and `(.1,.05,.05,.01,.55,.55)`. A deterministic coarse law cannot exactly represent both rates or source energies. Its minimax energy error over these two states is at least half their energy gap. A constant `epsilon_A` cannot repair this lost state. This proves an exact closure obstruction over a domain including both states, without excluding a future explicitly bounded local approximation.
