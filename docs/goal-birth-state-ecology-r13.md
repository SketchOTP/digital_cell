# Goal-mode birth-state ecology R13

## Largest unresolved causal gap

R12 proved that a shared finite extracellular medium can transfer conserved N/F,
but its inherited developmental founder fissioned at step 25 before contact at
step 418. The remaining gap is causal ordering: resource-dependent growth must
begin from the existing founder birth state, before any inherited fission event,
without changing the fission gate or material-flow law.

## Why this increment closes that gap

This opt-in replay starts the existing shared-medium contract at the exact
`initial_population(seed=2)` state. It uses the unchanged local exchange,
reaction, growth, mechanics, polarity, and fission code for the established
12,000-step horizon, with a transfer-disabled twin. No new pool, rate, gain,
threshold, controller, or resource geometry is introduced.

The local replay reaches contact and transfer at step 7627 before any fission.
The active arm remains alive with finite delivery and exact medium
conservation; the disabled arm receives zero material and dies of starvation.
Neither arm fissions within the fixed horizon, so resource-causal reproduction
remains unestablished.

## What this supersedes

This closes the R12 interpretation that inherited pre-resource fission was the
confound. It also collapses the need for another local material-flow variant:
the shared-medium contract is causally ordered before fission when ecology
starts at birth, yet the unchanged growth/fission composition still does not
produce reproduction.
