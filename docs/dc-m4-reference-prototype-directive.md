## Acceptance criteria

1. The program has explicit fixed illustrative parameters, material and sealed
   numerical budgets; exact rational inputs reject floats/nonfinite values and
   invalid domains. Public entry starts at L=L0, a=0, b=P, zero stores and finite A.
2. Finite prescribed conversion strokes reserve the full overhead plus uphill
   path variation, dissipate downhill variation, conserve A/W and polarity, and
   never recover A. A real sign-certified in-domain scalar root solver uses both
   reciprocal stores and the true piecewise stretch secant.
3. Root, force-residual, energy-defect and cumulative absolute-error certificates
   meet prospectively fixed reference budgets. Numerical defects remain visible
   diagnostics: no heat/store/fuel repair. Cold-reachable cycles and constructed
   local algebra controls have distinct claims. No floating roundoff claim is
   inferred from rational arithmetic.
4. Complete cycles are immutable transactions. Invalid funding/domain/type,
   missing bracket, exhausted iteration/accuracy budget or unsupported ports,
   material/topology/coefficient changes and live OFF transitions reject without
   partial state/ledger publication, including failure after a funded reaction.
5. The plan specifies independent source eligibility, raw provenance, physical
   species/unit mapping, fixed train/holdout roles, correlated uncertainty and
   tight independent accuracy budgets, failure rules and v2 work/heat accounting.
   Empty templates are labelled NOT_ACQUIRED, all four production values remain
   null and absent data give MISSING_EVIDENCE rather than calibration acceptance.
6. A distinct reviewer hash-binds final implementation, tests, documentation,
   acquisition plan/template, directive, verifier and workflow. Parent-owned
   substantive tests and exact-head hosted CI pass; downloaded artifact hashes,
   frozen inputs, prior checkouts/PRs/archives and final Notion readback verify.
