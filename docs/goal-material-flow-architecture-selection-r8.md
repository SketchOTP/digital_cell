# Goal-mode material-flow architecture selection R8

Status: `GOAL_AGENT_PROVISIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_SELECTION`

This is a source-level selection record after the R7 unified flux ledger. It
does not accept the assimilation state as project architecture and does not
authorize another organism-world runtime variant.

## Largest unresolved end-goal gap

The remaining causal gap is:

```text
finite spatial environmental material
→ sufficient local transfer
→ endogenous processing
→ sustained structural growth
→ resource-causal physical fission
```

R7 identifies environmental N/F transfer as the first measured divergence
from the qualified whole-membrane reproductive reference. The spatial arm
does process and grow from finite delivered material, but it does not reach
the unchanged `1.35 * birth_mass` fission gate.

## Source-level comparison

The two transfer paths share the same local transport terms:

```text
permeability(theta, N/F)
k_flux
membrane edge length
dt
boundary concentration - interior concentration
```

They do not expose the same boundary:

| Path | External boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified whole-membrane reference | Fixed boundary concentration `2.063914918930895`; all membrane segments eligible in the calibration. |
| Current spatial field | Finite cell-local concentration sampled at edge midpoints; only local field-supported edges request material; requests share finite cell inventory. |

At the first R7 checkpoint, the whole-membrane reference transferred
`5.2197561097164` N while the spatial path transferred
`0.5735320665105335` N. The spatial path therefore delivered about `10.99%`
of the reference transfer at that checkpoint. Its initial patch-cell
concentration is about `85.94%` of the whole-membrane boundary concentration,
so concentration alone does not explain the observed difference. The
remaining comparison is affected by local exposure, edge-to-cell sampling,
and finite per-cell allocation.

The reference and spatial runs also carry different total environmental
stocks in the R7 replay (`8192` versus `2043.3859906526618` N across the two
daughter systems). Therefore the ledger establishes the first observed
divergence in the tested compositions, but does not establish a normalized
universal transfer-capacity ceiling.

This is not a decision to add another material pool, transport layer, gain,
threshold, or inventory variant. It is a decision to stop implementation until
the next boundary is specified and shown to be materially different from the
current local-field/contact composition.

## Required next contract

Before any new organism-world runtime mechanism, the contract must specify:

1. finite ownership and exact debit of every environmental N/F unit;
2. the local spatial boundary through which material reaches the organism;
3. the temporal relationship between environmental arrival, processing, and
   structural incorporation;
4. how the contract preserves the unchanged reaction, growth, and fission
   laws;
5. controls that distinguish transfer-caused growth from inherited or
   pre-resource growth;
6. checkpoint, remesh, fission, lineage, rotation, and observer-validity
   preservation;
7. a stop condition proving that the work is not another field-placement,
   contact-allocation, assimilation-buffer, or active-work hill-climb.

If such a contract cannot be written without changing the frozen chemistry,
introducing an unledgered source, or adding another unmotivated intermediate,
the current organism-world material architecture is insufficient and local
implementation stops.
